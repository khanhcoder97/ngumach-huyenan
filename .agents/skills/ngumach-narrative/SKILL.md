Write scenes with concrete actions, competing wants and a change worth experiencing. Preserve meaningful awkwardness and genre imagery; avoid blanket English word bans or AI-authorship scoring for Vietnamese.

Treat player speech as selectable rather than prescribing the avatar’s gender, class, ancestry or inner thoughts. Distinguish observation, a character’s belief and established fact. New props used only as local cinematic dressing need no world coordinates; new tracked objects, locations or story flags need an explicit integration proposal.
